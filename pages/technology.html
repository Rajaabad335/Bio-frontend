It enables innovations in different sectors of the economy, such as agriculture, manufacturing, services, education, health, and government, by increasing productivity, efficiency, and competitiveness and hence economic growth.

IT AND ITES
Pakistan IT sector is among the fastest growing sectors of the economy and is emerging as an ideal IT destination in the world. The IT sector is providing a broad set of products and services to the world’s largest companies. Pakistan’s IT industry counts world’s largest entities among its regular clients.

TELECOM PARAMETERS

Pakistan’s journey towards high-speed mobile broadband services accelerated in 2014, when the country conducted a spectrum auction for NGMS and initiated the deployment of 3G and 4G infrastructure in the country for the first time. In the past 10 years the telecom sector has grown by leaps and bounds and the broadband penetration now stands at 54.97%, a bulk of which is through mobiles services.

MOBILE BANKING

Since the introduction of 3G/4G technology, the country has seen a number of auxiliary services like mobile banking, branchless banking, digital payment infrastructure and other digital financial services develop and grow in the country.

MOBILE MANUFACTURING AND ASSEMBLY

Pakistan's mobile phone manufacturing industry has grown significantly in recent years as local manufacturing plants produced 10.87 million handsets in the first eight months of 2023.The locally manufactured/assembled 10.87 million mobile handsets included 7.62 million 2G and 3.04 million smartphones. 

Government’s mobile device manufacturing (MDM) policy has attracted at least 36 companies to manufacture/assemble smartphones under the policies introduced by the government in 2020.
