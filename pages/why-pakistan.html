WHY PAKISTAN

Pakistan is world famous for its hospitality and is diverse both in terms of ethnicities and topography. Pakistani culture represents peace, hospitality and humanity based social ethos across the country. 

Spread over 796,095 km², the country has Islamabad as the Federal Capital, four provinces Balochistan, KP, Punjab, Sindh and two federal territories: Azad Jammu and Kashmir and Gilgit Baltistan.

 From the mighty alpine stretches of the Karakorum in the North to the vast alluvial delta of the Indus River in the South, Pakistan remains a land of high adventure and nature. 

Trekking, mountaineering, white water rafting, hunting, mountain desert jeep safaris, camel and yak safaris, trout fishing and bird watching, are a few activities, which entice the adventure and nature lovers to Pakistan. 

Pakistan is home to five of the world’s fourteen mountains taller than 8000 meters (26000 feet), which also includes the second highest peak in the world, K2. This has made Pakistan a mountaineering haven for professional mountaineers.
